Pacing & safety settings
Staying safe. WhatsApp bans numbers that behave like bots: many messages in a short time, identical text, messages to people who never saved your number, and recipients tapping Report or Block. Keep the random delay, keep batches small, stay well under 200 messages a day on a personal number (far less on a brand-new one), write a message people expect from you, and let them reply or opt out. Prefer sending to people who already have your number saved. Unofficial automation is against WhatsApp's terms; for large volumes use the official WhatsApp Business Platform.
